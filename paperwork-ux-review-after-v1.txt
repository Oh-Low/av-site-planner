Locked v1 decisions (summary)
-----------------------------
- Right panel: Inspector top, Library below; collapsible with >; viewport grows without jumping pan/zoom.
- Left panel: paper settings, sheet tree, sheet title/notes; Generate packet + Update linked elements.
- Update linked elements: all sheets; keep positions, sizes, crops, and other element parameters.
- Library: by calculator, then by instance family; one entry per instance; hidden if unavailable; drag to page; ghost outline; drop at pointer; clamp on page.
- Toolbar above viewport only; Google Slides–like; style strip (fill/stroke/weight/font) before and after draw.
- Tools (extensible): Select, Text, Heading, Line, Polyline, Arrow, Rectangle, Ellipse, Bring forward, Send backward.
- Text: single-click default box. Line/arrow: click-drag. Rect/ellipse: click-drag or click for default. Polyline: click vertices, double-click/Enter to finish.
- Tools are one-shot (return to select). Pan/zoom unchanged while drawing (scroll zoom, right-drag pan).
- Decorations: packet-level list; "Show on all sheets" (default off); can hide on specific sheets; shared edits update all; above sheet elements by default.
- Auto sheets remain editable with these tools.
- Print includes drawings. Undo/redo and multi-select deferred.
